### Question
Why should the Ideal Gas Law NOT be used to determine steam properties?

### Explanation
The characteristic equation of state for a vapor is considerably more complicated than for an ideal gas, so steam tables (built from extensive experimental measurement) are used instead.

### Question
What region on a P-v diagram is called the 'vapor dome'?

### Explanation
The vapor dome (wet vapor region) is the area bounded by the saturated liquid and saturated vapor lines, where a substance can exist in equilibrium as both liquid and gas.

### Question
What happens to a saturated liquid's temperature as heat is added during vaporization?

### Explanation

What is supersaturation?

### Explanation
