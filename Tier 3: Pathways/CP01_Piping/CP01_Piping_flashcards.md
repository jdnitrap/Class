# CP01_Piping - Quick Reference Flashcards

## Flashcard ID: 1
### Question: What is the primary purpose of piping systems?

Transport fluids/fluid-solid mixtures between equipment. Key factors: temperature, pressure, flow rate, shock loads.

---

## Flashcard ID: 2
### Question: What five design factors affect pipe material selection?

Temperature, pressure, flow rate/velocity, shock loads (water/steam hammer, thermal shock), directional changes.

---

## Flashcard ID: 3
### Question: Which piping material is economical but prone to rust and corrosion?

Low-carbon steel.

---

## Flashcard ID: 4
### Question: What material is best for high-temperature and high-pressure applications?

Nickel alloys (high-cost) or low-alloy steel.

---

## Flashcard ID: 5
### Question: What are the three types of pipe support systems?

Rigid restraints (fixed), spring hangers (allow thermal movement), snubbers (limit seismic motion).

---

## Flashcard ID: 6
### Question: How do piping systems accommodate thermal expansion?

Thermal loops/bends or expansion joints allow controlled linear expansion.

---

## Flashcard ID: 7
### Question: What are the five principal valve functions?

Start/stop flow, throttle flow, control direction, regulate pressure, relieve pressure.

---

## Flashcard ID: 8
### Question: What type of valve is used to start or stop complete fluid flow?

Gate valves, ball valves, or plug valves.

---

## Flashcard ID: 9
### Question: What type of valve controls flow rate without completely stopping flow?

Globe valves or needle valves.

---

## Flashcard ID: 10
### Question: Name three basic valve construction components.

Body (flow path/seats), bonnet (sealing section), disk (opens/closes/throttles).

---

## Flashcard ID: 11
### Question: What is the purpose of the backseat in a valve?

Allows packing adjustment without depressurizing system.

---

## Flashcard ID: 12
### Question: What is the difference between rising-stem and non-rising stem valves?

Rising-stem: moves upward (visual indicator). Non-rising: position not visually obvious.

---

## Flashcard ID: 13
### Question: What is pressure defined as?

Pressure = Force / Area (P = F/A). Units: psi, Pa, bar, atm.

---

## Flashcard ID: 14
### Question: What is normal atmospheric pressure in psia and kPa?

14.7 psia = 101.3 kPa.

---

## Flashcard ID: 15
### Question: What is hydrostatic pressure?

Pressure from fluid column weight above reference point (P = ρ × g × h).

---

## Flashcard ID: 16
### Question: What is absolute pressure used for?

Thermodynamic calculations; measured from perfect vacuum. Example: 10 psig = 24.7 psia.

---

## Flashcard ID: 17
### Question: What is gage pressure (PSIG)?

Pressure above atmospheric (Pgage = Pabs - Patm). Common gauge reading.

---

## Flashcard ID: 18
### Question: What is differential pressure (PSID)?

Difference between two locations. Used for: filter drop, valve throttling, level measurement.

---

## Flashcard ID: 19
### Question: What does Pascal's Law state?

Pressure in confined fluid transmits undiminished in all directions (F1/A1 = F2/A2).

---

## Flashcard ID: 20
### Question: What is thermal binding in valves?

Body cools faster than disk → differential contraction binds seat to disk → valve may not reopen.

---

## Flashcard ID: 21
### Question: How can thermal binding be prevented?

Crack valve open/re-shut during cooldown. Use proper closing torque. Allow gradual cooling.

---

## Flashcard ID: 22
### Question: What is pressure locking in valves?

Liquid trapped in bonnet pressurizes when heated → prevents valve opening (high-temp systems).

---

## Flashcard ID: 23
### Question: How is pressure locking prevented?

Provide relief or vent path on bonnet to equalize pressure buildup.

---

## Flashcard ID: 24
### Question: Should a wrench ever be used on a valve handwheel for extra leverage?

No. Can overstress actuator gearing/casing → catastrophic failure.

---

## Flashcard ID: 25
### Question: What does reaching hard stop on a handwheel confirm?

Nothing. Must verify full travel by counting designed turns; position limit switch is unreliable.
