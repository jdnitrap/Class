### Question
Which of the following is NOT one of the five principal functions of valves?

### Explanation
The five principal valve functions are: starting/stopping, throttling, controlling flow direction, regulating pressure, and relieving pressure. Increasing system pressure is not a valve function.

### Question
What is thermal binding in valves?

### Explanation
Thermal binding occurs when the valve body cools faster than the disk, causing the disk to bind against the seat. Prevention: crack open periodically during cooldown.

### Question
What is pressure locking in a valve?

### Explanation
Pressure locking occurs when trapped liquid in the bonnet heats and pressurizes, making the valve impossible to open. Prevention: provide bonnet relief or vent paths.
