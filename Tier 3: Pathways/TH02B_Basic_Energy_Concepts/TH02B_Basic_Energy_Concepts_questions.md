### Question
What does the First Law of Thermodynamics state?

### Explanation
The First Law of Thermodynamics is the rule of conservation of energy: energy can't be created or destroyed, only transformed, and energy in must equal energy stored plus energy out.

### Question
Which of the following is a form of STORED energy?

### Explanation
The four forms of stored energy are potential, kinetic, internal, and flow energy. Heat and work are transient energy forms.

### Question
What is enthalpy?

### Explanation
Enthalpy (H) is a measure of a fluid's energy content due to temperature, pressure, and volume, equal to internal energy plus PV energy: H = U + PV.
