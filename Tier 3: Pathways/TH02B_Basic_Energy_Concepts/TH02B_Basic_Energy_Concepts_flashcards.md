### Question
What is the First Law of Thermodynamics?

### Answer
Conservation of energy -- energy can neither be created nor destroyed, only transformed between forms; energy entering a system must equal energy stored in or leaving it.

---
id: 329
---
### Question
What are the two general categories of energy in thermodynamics?

### Answer
Stored energy (energy contained by a mass due to its properties) and transient energy (energy associated with changing one stored energy form into another).

---
id: 330
---
### Question
Name the four forms of stored energy.

### Answer
Potential energy, kinetic energy, internal energy, and flow energy.

---
id: 331
---
### Question
Name the two forms of transient energy.

### Answer
Heat and work.

---
id: 332
---
### Question
What does potential energy depend on?

### Answer
Mass and elevation (height) relative to a reference point.

---
id: 333
---
### Question
What does kinetic energy depend on?

### Answer
Mass and velocity.

---
id: 334
---
### Question
What is internal energy?

### Answer
The sum of all microscopic energy forms in a fluid, due to rotation, vibration, translation, and molecular interactions.

---
id: 335
---
### Question
What is specific internal energy?

### Answer
Internal energy per unit mass (u = U/m), in BTU/lbm.

---
id: 336
---
### Question
What is flow energy?

### Answer
The energy a fluid possesses due to its pressure and volume -- also called P-energy or flow work.

---
id: 337
---
### Question
State the Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy.

### Answer
For a frictionless system, the sum of potential and kinetic energy is constant.

---
id: 338
---
### Question
What is heat?

### Answer
Energy in transition due to a temperature difference; it flows from hotter to colder, with no flow if there's no temperature difference.

---
id: 339
---
### Question
What is specific heat capacity?

### Answer
The energy needed to raise 1 lbm of a substance's temperature by 1F, in BTU/lbm-F.

---
id: 340
---
### Question
What is water's specific heat capacity at STP?

### Answer
1.0 BTU/lbm-F.

---
id: 341
---
### Question
What is work, and how is it calculated?

### Answer
Transient energy from moving a mass some distance; W = F x d (force times distance).

---
id: 342
---
### Question
What is the difference between mechanical work and flow work?

### Answer
Mechanical work moves a solid object a distance (force x distance); flow work moves a flowing fluid (pressure x volume).

---
id: 343
---
### Question
What is the equation for flow work?

### Answer
WFlow = PV (pressure times volume).

---
id: 344
---
### Question
What is power?

### Answer
The rate of doing work -- work done per unit of time, measured in horsepower or watts.

---
id: 345
---
### Question
How much work per second can a 50-horsepower engine provide?

### Answer
27,500 ft-lbf per second.

id: 346
---
### Question
What is enthalpy?

### Answer
A measure of a fluid's energy content due to temperature, pressure, and volume -- the sum of internal energy (U) and PV energy: H = U + PV.

---
id: 347
---
### Question
What is specific enthalpy?

### Answer
h = u + Pv (specific internal energy plus specific flow energy), in BTU/lbm.

---
id: 348
---
### Question
What is entropy?

### Answer
A measure of the unavailable energy in a fluid at a given state, expressing the amount of disorder in a system.

---
id: 349
---
### Question
What is the General Energy Equation for a closed system with no PE/KE change?

### Answer
Change in enthalpy (h) = heat transferred to the system (q) - work done by the system (w).

---
id: 350
---
### Question
In the General Energy Equation's sign convention, when is q positive?

### Answer
When heat is added to the system (negative if heat is removed).

---
id: 351
---
### Question
In the General Energy Equation's sign convention, when is w positive?

### Answer
When work is done BY the system (negative if work is done ON the system).

---
id: 352
---
### Question
What conversion factor is used to express mechanical energy (ft-lbf) in BTUs?

### Answer
Joule's constant, J = 778 ft-lbf/BTU.

---
id: 353
---
### Question
For a steady-flow system, what does the rate-form of the General Energy Equation's work term represent?

### Answer
The power output of the system.
