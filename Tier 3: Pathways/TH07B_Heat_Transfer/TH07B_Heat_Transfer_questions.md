## Question ID: 147
### Question: What is the definition of heat transfer?

**Options:**
- a) The transfer of energy that occurs as a result of a temperature difference
- b) The movement of mass between systems
- c) The conversion of work to heat only
- d) The storage of thermal energy in a substance

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** a

**Explanation:** Heat transfer is the transfer of energy due to a temperature difference, from higher to lower temperature.

## Question ID: 148
### Question: Which of the following is NOT one of the three basic modes of heat transfer?

**Options:**
- a) Conduction
- b) Convection
- c) Radiation
- d) Evaporation

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** d

**Explanation:** The three basic modes are conduction, convection, and radiation. Evaporation is a phase change process, not a mode of heat transfer.

**Correct:** a

## Question ID: 152
### Question: What is the overall heat transfer coefficient (Uo) in a heat exchanger?

**Options:**

- b) The thermal conductivity of the tube material only
- c) The convection coefficient of the hot fluid only

## Question ID: 154

**Options:**

- b) It cannot occur in the presence of water
- c) It is too efficient and would overheat the core
