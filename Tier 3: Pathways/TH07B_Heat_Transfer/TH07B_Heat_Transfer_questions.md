Which of the following is NOT one of the three basic modes of heat transfer?

### Question
In the heat exchanger performance equation for single-phase fluids, Q = m × cp × ΔT, what does a negative Q indicate?

### Explanation
Negative Q means heat loss from the fluid; positive means heat gain. The other fluid has the opposite sign.
