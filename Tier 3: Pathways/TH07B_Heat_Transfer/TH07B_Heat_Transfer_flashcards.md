# TH07B_Heat_Transfer - Quick Reference Flashcards

## Flashcard ID: 1
### What are the three basic modes of heat transfer?

Conduction (molecular), Convection (fluid motion), Radiation (EM waves).

---

## Flashcard ID: 2
### Which heat transfer modes dominate in PWR plants?

Conduction and convection. Radiation negligible normal op, significant in accidents.

---

## Flashcard ID: 3
### What is specific heat capacity (cp) and its formula?

Heat to raise unit mass one degree. Q = m × cp × ΔT. 1 Btu raises 1 lbm water by 1°F.

---

## Flashcard ID: 4
### What is conduction equation and what affects it?

Q = kAΔT / x. Higher k or A increases rate. Higher x decreases rate. Metals good, gases/ceramics poor.

---

## Flashcard ID: 5
### Which materials have high thermal conductivity (k)?

Copper (133.0), Iron (39.0), 90-10 CuNi (23) Btu/hr-ft-°F. Metals conduct well.

---

## Flashcard ID: 6
### Which materials have low thermal conductivity?

Air (0.0145), Rock Wool (0.03), Concrete (0.2-0.7) Btu/hr-ft-°F. Gases/insulation poor.

---

## Flashcard ID: 7
### What is convection?

Heat transfer between fluid and surface via circulation/mixing. Boundary layer = thin film at surface.

---

## Flashcard ID: 8
### What is natural vs forced convection?

Natural: density-driven. Forced: pump-driven (more efficient). Plants use forced convection.

---

## Flashcard ID: 9
### What is boundary layer and what affects it?

Thin stagnant film at surface. Affected by: temperature ↑ thins layer, flow ↑ thins layer, roughness.

---

## Flashcard ID: 10
### What is heat transfer coefficient (hf)?

hf = kf / xf (boundary layer conductivity / thickness). Higher hf = better heat transfer.

---

## Flashcard ID: 11
### What are the three layers in heat exchanger transfer?

Hot fluid convection → tube wall conduction → cold fluid convection. All in series.

---

## Flashcard ID: 12
### What does overall heat transfer coefficient (Uo) represent?

Uo = 1 / total resistance. Q = Uo × Ao × ΔTo.

---

## Flashcard ID: 13
### How does fouling affect heat transfer coefficient?

Adds extra resistance layer. 2 mil scale can drop Uo ~30%. Monitor to detect degradation.

---

## Flashcard ID: 14
### What is radiation heat transfer?

Transfers heat via EM waves (no medium). Stefan-Boltzmann: Q = σ × A × ε × T⁴.

---

## Flashcard ID: 15
### Why is radiation negligible in normal PWR operation?

Requires very high temp difference (T⁴ term). Critical in accidents only.

---

## Flashcard ID: 16
### What is single-phase heat transfer?

Both fluids remain liquid OR gas (not mixed). Equation: Q = m × cp × ΔT.

---

## Flashcard ID: 17
### What is two-phase heat transfer?

One fluid changes phase (condenser, steam generator). Equation: Q = m × Δh.

---

## Flashcard ID: 18
### What does CTP represent?

Core Thermal Power. Heat input rate from core to Reactor Coolant System.

---

## Flashcard ID: 19
### What are the two methods to calculate CTP?

RCS heat balance: Q = m × cp × ΔT. Secondary heat balance: Q = m × Δh (steam - feedwater).

---

## Flashcard ID: 20
### What is condensate depression and optimal range?

Tsat - Tcondensate. Prevents pump cavitation. Optimal: 10-20°F below saturation.
