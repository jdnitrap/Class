What is the term for steam that is 100% vapor at its boiling temperature, with no liquid remaining?

### Explanation

Heat that changes a substance's phase without changing its temperature is called:

### Explanation
Latent heat is phase-change heat (melting, vaporization) that doesn't change temperature. Sensible heat does change temperature.

What term describes the fraction of vapor present in a wet steam mixture?

### Explanation

### Question
According to Charles' Law, at constant pressure, the volume of a gas is:

### Explanation
Charles' Law: V ∝ T at constant P. V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ (using absolute temperature).

According to Boyle's Law, at constant temperature, the volume of a gas is:

### Question
Why should the Ideal Gas Law not be used to determine the properties of steam?

### Explanation
Steam deviates significantly from ideal gas behavior, especially near saturation. Steam tables provide experimentally accurate properties.

What does the 'void fraction' represent in two-phase flow?

On a P-T diagram, what is the significance of the Critical Point?

### Explanation

### Question
What are 'degrees of superheat' in a thermodynamic process?

### Explanation
Degrees of superheat = T_actual - T_saturation. For example, if steam is at 350°F when saturation is 300°F, superheat = 50°F.

### Question
What are 'degrees of subcooling' for a liquid?

### Explanation
Degrees of subcooling = T_saturation - T_actual. Condensate at 100°F when saturation is 110°F has 10°F of subcooling.

### Question
Why does cavitation occur in a pump?

### Explanation
Cavitation: local pressure < saturation pressure → vapor bubbles form → downstream, pressure increases → bubbles collapse violently → erosion and vibration.
