### Question
How is the state of a substance defined?

### Answer
By two independent intensive properties. Know any two → determine all others.

---
id: 2
---
### Question
What are the four phases of matter?

### Answer
Solid (fixed shape/volume), Liquid (fixed volume, container shape), Vapor/Gas (no fixed shape/volume), Plasma (ionized).

---
id: 3
---
### Question
What is melting and vaporization?

### Answer
Melting: Solid → Liquid (absorbs heat). Vaporization: Liquid → Gas (absorbs heat).

---
id: 4
---
### Question
What is condensation and solidification?

### Answer
Condensation: Gas → Liquid (rejects heat). Solidification: Liquid → Solid (rejects heat).

---
id: 5
---
### Question
What are the three boundary lines on phase diagram?

### Answer
Fusion (Solid-Liquid), Vaporization (Liquid-Gas), Sublimation (Solid-Gas).

---
id: 6
---
### Question
What is the triple point on phase diagram?

### Answer
Point where all three phases coexist in equilibrium.

---
id: 7
---
### Question
What are the five states of water?

### Answer
Subcooled liquid, Saturated liquid, Wet vapor (mixture), Saturated vapor, Superheated vapor.

---
id: 8
---
### Question
What is subcooled liquid?

### Answer
Below saturation temp. Resistant to vaporization. Nearly incompressible.

---
id: 9
---
### Question
What is saturated liquid and saturated vapor?

### Answer
Saturated liquid: at saturation temp, about to boil. Saturated vapor: 100% vapor, last drop evaporated.

---
id: 10
---
### Question
What is wet vapor?

### Answer
Mixture of liquid + vapor at same temperature.

---
id: 11
---
### Question
What is sensible heat?

### Answer
Heat that changes temperature without phase change.

---
id: 12
---
### Question
What is latent heat?

### Answer
Heat that causes phase change at constant temperature.

---
id: 13
---
### Question
What is latent heat of vaporization (Hfg)?

### Answer
Energy to convert saturated liquid → saturated vapor. Released on condensation.

---
id: 14
---
### Question
What is steam quality (X)?

### Answer
Fraction of vapor in wet mixture. Range: 0 (all liquid) to 1.0 (all vapor).

---
id: 15
---
### Question
What quality steam is required at turbine inlet and why?

### Answer
High quality (>95-99%) to prevent blade erosion and corrosion.
