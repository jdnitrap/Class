### Question
What is mass flow rate and its formula?

### Answer
ṁ = ρ × V̇ = ρ × v × A (lbm/hr). ρ = density, v = velocity, A = area.

---
id: 2
---
### Question
What is volumetric flow rate and common units?

### Answer
Q = v × A. Units: gpm (gallons per minute) or cfm (cubic feet per minute).

---
id: 3
---
### Question
What is continuity equation?

### Answer
Mass in = Mass out. ṁ₁ = ṁ₂.

---
id: 4
---
### Question
What is viscosity and how does temperature affect it?

### Answer
Fluid's resistance to flow. Liquids: viscosity ↓ with temp. Gases: viscosity ↑ with temp.

---
id: 5
---
### Question
What is specific gravity (SG)?

### Answer
Density ratio vs water @ 60°F. Water = 1.0. SG < 1 floats, SG > 1 sinks.

---
id: 6
---
### Question
What is fluid density and typical value for water?

### Answer
Mass per unit volume. Water ≈ 62.4 lbm/ft³ (standard). Changes with temp/pressure.

### Question
What is laminar flow?

### Answer
Re < 2,000. Smooth layers, minimal mixing. Parabolic velocity profile.

---
id: 8
---
### Question
What is turbulent flow?

### Answer
Re > 3,500. Cross-currents, mixing. Flattened velocity profile. Requires more energy.

---
id: 9
---
### Question
What is transitional flow?

### Answer
Re = 2,000-3,500. Unstable. Alternates between laminar and turbulent.

---
id: 10
---
### Question
What is Reynolds number and its formula?

### Answer
NRe = (ρ × v × d) / μ (dimensionless). Determines flow regime.

---
id: 11
---
### Question
What is single-phase flow?

### Answer
Fluid either liquid OR gas, not mixed. Easier to analyze and predict.

---
id: 12
---
### Question
What is two-phase flow?

### Answer
Liquid AND vapor mixture. Greater resistance. Head loss increases 100×+.

---
id: 13
---
### Question
What is elevation head (z)?

### Answer
Potential energy in Bernoulli. Height above reference. Path-independent.

---
id: 14
---
### Question
What is velocity head?

### Answer
Kinetic energy in Bernoulli. v²/(2gc). Height if KE converted to PE.

---
id: 15
---
### Question
What is pressure head (Pv)?

### Answer
Pressure-volume energy. Height of column weighing = fluid pressure.

---
id: 16
---
### Question
What is total head?

### Answer
Elevation + velocity + pressure heads. Constant in ideal flow.

---
id: 17
---
### Question
What is friction head loss (Hf)?

### Answer
Pressure/velocity → heat through friction. Expressed in feet of head.

---
id: 18
---
### Question
What is friction factor (f)?

### Answer
Dimensionless Darcy constant. Accounts for pipe roughness. Depends on Re.

---
id: 19
---
### Question
What is cavitation?

### Answer
Vapor bubbles when pressure < saturation pressure. Damages pump impellers.

---
id: 20
---
### Question
What is choked flow?

### Answer
Compressible flow (steam) reaches Mach 1. Limits mass flow. Pout/Pin ≈ 0.5.

---
id: 21
---
### Question
What is water hammer?

### Answer
Sudden flow change → pressure surges. Can spike to 20× normal. Pipe damage, injury risk.

---
id: 22
---
### Question
What is pump head (Hp)?

### Answer
Energy added by pump. Wp = ṁ × Hp × gc/g.

---
id: 23
---
### Question
What is continuity for variable pipe diameter?

### Answer
v₁ × A₁ = v₂ × A₂. Smaller diameter = higher velocity.

---
id: 24
---
### Question
How does velocity change with pipe diameter?

### Answer
Diameter ↑ → velocity ↓. Diameter ↓ → velocity ↑. Proportional.

### Question
What is Bernoulli's equation?

### Answer
z₁ + v₁²/(2gc) + Pv₁ = z₂ + v₂²/(2gc) + Pv₂. Total head constant.
