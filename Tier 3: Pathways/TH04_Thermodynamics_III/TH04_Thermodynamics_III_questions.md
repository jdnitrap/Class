### Question
In a process where no heat is transferred to or from the system, what term describes this condition?

### Explanation
Adiabatic process: Q = 0 (no heat transfer). Temperature and pressure can change in adiabatic processes.

### Question
For a turbine, the work output is typically expressed as the difference between which two values?

### Explanation
Turbine work: W = h_in - h_out. The enthalpy drop across the turbine equals the mechanical work output.

What does the term 'isenthalpic' describe in a thermodynamic process?

An ideal turbine or pump work process is described as isentropic because:

### Question
What process occurs when steam flows through a partially open valve, resulting in pressure drop and entropy increase?

### Explanation
Throttling: constant enthalpy (h_in = h_out), pressure drops, entropy increases. No work done, no heat added. Used for flow control.

What is pump efficiency defined as?

### Explanation
Pump efficiency = ideal (isentropic) work / real (shaft) work. Real pumps require more shaft power than ideal due to friction.

### Question
How does superheating steam in the boiler affect turbine work output?

### Explanation
Superheating increases the enthalpy of steam entering the turbine (h_in increases), resulting in greater work output (W = h_in - h_out).

### Question
What is the effect of friction losses in a real turbine compared to an ideal isentropic turbine?

### Explanation
Friction in real turbines increases entropy (ΔS > 0), reducing the enthalpy drop available for work. Turbine efficiency accounts for this.
