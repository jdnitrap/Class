### Question
What is an adiabatic process?

### Answer
No heat transfer (Q = 0). Boundary insulated. Common in turbines/pumps.

---
id: 2
---
### Question
What is an isobaric process?

### Answer
Constant pressure. Heat added/removed at constant pressure. Boiler, condenser.

---
id: 3
---
### Question
What is an isentropic process?

### Answer
No entropy change. Reversible adiabatic. Theoretical ideal for efficiency benchmarks.

---
id: 4
---
### Question
What is isenthalpic process (throttling)?

### Answer
No enthalpy change. Occurs in valve/orifice. Pressure drops, entropy increases.

---
id: 5
---
### Question
What is an isothermal process?

### Answer
Constant temperature. System in thermal equilibrium.

---
id: 6
---
### Question
What are the three requirements for steady-flow?

### Answer
Constant mass flow (m_in = m_out). Constant properties at each point. Constant heat/work rates.

---
id: 7
---
### Question
What is the mechanical equivalent of heat?

### Answer
1 Btu = 778 ft-lbf = 1055 Joules.

---
id: 8
---
### Question
What does Second Law of Thermodynamics state?

### Answer
No engine converts all heat to work. Heat rejection required. Max efficiency depends only on temps.

---
id: 9
---
### Question
What is Carnot efficiency formula?

### Answer
η = (T_hot - T_cold) / T_hot = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot).

---
id: 10
---
### Question
What is entropy change formula?

### Answer
ΔS = Q / T (Btu/lbm-R). More heat at low temp = larger entropy change.

---
id: 11
---
### Question
What is the energy equation for turbines?

### Answer
W = h_in - h_out. (Enthalpy drop = work output).

---
id: 12
---
### Question
What is the energy equation for pumps?

### Answer
W = h_out - h_in. (Work input = enthalpy rise).

---
id: 13
---
### Question
What does a boiler (steam generator) do?

### Answer
Raises feedwater enthalpy to produce steam. Isobaric heat addition.

---
id: 14
---
### Question
What does a condenser do?

### Answer
Removes latent heat. Collapses steam. Maintains vacuum. Improves turbine efficiency.

---
id: 15
---
### Question
Why is condensate subcooling important?

### Answer
Prevents pump cavitation. Optimal: 10-20°F below saturation.

---
id: 16
---
### Question
What is nozzle function?

### Answer
Converts pressure/enthalpy → velocity/kinetic energy through shaped passages.

---
id: 17
---
### Question
What are convergent and divergent nozzles?

### Answer
Convergent: area ↓, velocity ↑, pressure ↓. Divergent: area ↑, velocity ↓, pressure ↑.

### Question
What is turbine efficiency?

### Answer
Ratio of actual work to ideal work. Typically 80-90%.

---
id: 19
---
### Question
What is pump efficiency?

### Answer
Ratio of ideal work to actual work (opposite of turbine). Typically 75-85%.

---
id: 20
---
### Question
What is net cycle work?

### Answer
W_net = W_turbine - W_pump. Pump work only 1-5% of turbine work.
