# TH01_Measurement - Quick Reference Flashcards

## Flashcard ID: 1
### What are the units for force and mass in American Engineering System?

Force: lbf (pounds force), Mass: lbm (pounds mass).

---

## Flashcard ID: 2
### What temperature scale is used in American Engineering System?

Fahrenheit (°F).

---

## Flashcard ID: 3
### What is the conversion factor for work in American system?

Btu = (lbf × ft) / 778.

---

## Flashcard ID: 4
### What are the main units in SI/Metric System?

Force: N, Mass: kg, Temperature: °C or K, Pressure: Pa/kPa/bar.

---

## Flashcard ID: 5
### What is the freezing point of water in °F and °C?

32°F = 0°C.

---

## Flashcard ID: 6
### What is the boiling point of water in °F and °C?

212°F = 100°C.

---

## Flashcard ID: 7
### What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

°C = (°F - 32) / 1.8.

---

## Flashcard ID: 8
### What is Absolute Zero in Rankine and Kelvin?

0°R = -459.67°F. 0 K = -273.15°C.

---

## Flashcard ID: 9
### What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Rankine?

°R = °F + 459.67.

---

## Flashcard ID: 10
### What is the formula to convert Celsius to Kelvin?

K = °C + 273.15.

---

## Flashcard ID: 11
### Why are absolute temperature scales required in thermodynamics?

All thermodynamic equations require absolute temperature; relative scales produce wrong results.

---

## Flashcard ID: 12
### What is a system in thermodynamics?

A quantity of matter or space separated from surroundings by a boundary.

---

## Flashcard ID: 13
### What is an isolated system?

No mass transfer, no energy transfer. Theoretical ideal only.

---

## Flashcard ID: 14
### What is a closed system?

No mass transfer. Energy transfer (heat/work) allowed. Most common for analysis.

---

## Flashcard ID: 15
### What is an open system (control volume)?

Mass transfer across boundary (inlet/outlet flows). Energy transfer allowed.

---
