## Question ID: 21
### Question: According to Pascal's Law, a pressure change applied to an enclosed, incompressible fluid is transmitted:

**Options:**
- a) Only at the point of application
- b) Undiminished to all points within the fluid and to the walls of the container
- c) Only downward due to gravity
- d) Only along the path of least resistance

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** b

**Explanation:** Pascal's Law: pressure applied to an enclosed fluid transmits undiminished throughout in all directions.

## Question ID: 22
### Question: Gauge pressure plus atmospheric pressure equals which pressure value?

**Options:**
- a) Vacuum pressure
- b) Differential pressure
- c) Absolute pressure
- d) Static pressure

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** c

**Explanation:** Pabs = Pgage + Patm. Absolute pressure is always the sum of gauge and atmospheric pressures.

### Question: Specific volume is best defined as:

**Options:**
- a) The total volume of a substance
- b) The volume occupied by a unit mass of a substance (the reciprocal of density)
- c) The rate of volume change
- d) The volume at standard temperature and pressure

**Correct:** b

## Question ID: 24
### Question: What is the primary difference between a closed system and an open system in thermodynamics?

**Options:**
- a) A closed system allows energy transfer across its boundary but not mass transfer, while an open system allows transfer of both mass and energy
- b) A closed system allows no transfer of mass or energy
- c) An open system is always at constant pressure
- d) A closed system is only used in laboratories

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** a

**Explanation:** Closed systems have fixed mass boundaries but allow energy exchange. Open systems (control volumes) allow both mass and energy crossing boundaries.

---

## Question ID: 25
### Question: Which type of thermodynamic property depends on the amount of substance present, such as volume or weight?

**Options:**
- a) Intensive property
- b) Extensive property
- c) Derived property
- d) Critical property

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** b

**Explanation:** Extensive properties (volume, mass, weight) depend on the amount of substance. Intensive properties (pressure, temperature) do not.

**Options:**
- a) Celsius

## Question ID: 27
### Question: Which pressure measurement indicates a pressure below atmospheric pressure?

**Options:**
- a) Gauge pressure
- b) Absolute pressure
- c) Vacuum pressure
- d) Differential pressure

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** c

**Explanation:** Vacuum pressure is any pressure below atmospheric. Pvac = Patm - Pabs.

## Question ID: 28
### Question: What term describes the difference in pressure between two locations, such as across a filter?

**Options:**
- a) Static pressure
- b) Dynamic pressure
- c) Differential pressure
- d) Transient pressure

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** c

**Explanation:** Differential pressure (psid) is the pressure difference between two points in a system, commonly used across filters and restrictors.

## Question ID: 29
### Question: In a hydraulic press, if the output piston has a larger area than the input piston, how does the output force compare to the input force?

**Options:**
- a) The output force is less than the input force
- b) The output force equals the input force
- c) The output force is greater than the input force
- d) Output force depends only on fluid type

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** c

**Explanation:** In a hydraulic press, F = P × A. A larger output piston area produces greater output force (Pascal's Law application).

## Question ID: 30
### Question: Why does liquid temperature have a much greater effect on density and specific volume than pressure does?

**Options:**
- a) Because liquids expand more with heat
- b) Because liquids are relatively incompressible, so pressure has little effect on their volume
- c) Because pressure always increases density
- d) Because temperature is more important than pressure

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** b

**Explanation:** Liquids are nearly incompressible, so pressure changes have minimal volume effect. Temperature changes cause greater density variations.

## Question ID: 80
### Question: Which of the following is an example of an open (control volume) system?

**Options:**
- a) A sealed cylinder with a fixed amount of gas
- b) A steam turbine where steam enters and exits
- c) A closed tank with no inlets or outlets
- d) A battery converting chemical energy

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** b

## Question ID: 82
### Question: Hydrostatic pressure in a fluid column is proportional to which of the following?

**Options:**
- a) Only the fluid temperature
- b) The fluid's density and the height of the column above the reference point
- c) Only the fluid's specific volume
- d) The container shape

**Correct:** a
**Correct:** b

**Explanation:** Hydrostatic pressure P = ρgh, where ρ is density, g is gravitational acceleration, and h is height above reference.

- d) Dyne
