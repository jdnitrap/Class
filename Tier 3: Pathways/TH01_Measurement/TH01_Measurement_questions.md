Gauge pressure plus atmospheric pressure equals which pressure value?

### Explanation
Pabs = Pgage + Patm. Absolute pressure is always the sum of gauge and atmospheric pressures.

### Question
What is the primary difference between a closed system and an open system in thermodynamics?

### Explanation
Closed systems have fixed mass boundaries but allow energy exchange. Open systems (control volumes) allow both mass and energy crossing boundaries.

### Question
Which type of thermodynamic property depends on the amount of substance present, such as volume or weight?

### Explanation
Extensive properties (volume, mass, weight) depend on the amount of substance. Intensive properties (pressure, temperature) do not.

### Question
In a hydraulic press, if the output piston has a larger area than the input piston, how does the output force compare to the input force?

### Explanation
In a hydraulic press, F = P × A. A larger output piston area produces greater output force (Pascal's Law application).

### Question
Why does liquid temperature have a much greater effect on density and specific volume than pressure does?

### Explanation
Liquids are nearly incompressible, so pressure changes have minimal volume effect. Temperature changes cause greater density variations.

### Question
Hydrostatic pressure in a fluid column is proportional to which of the following?

### Explanation
Hydrostatic pressure P = ρgh, where ρ is density, g is gravitational acceleration, and h is height above reference.

### Question
In the American Engineering System (English System), what is the standard unit for force?

### Explanation
The American Engineering System uses pound-force (lbf) for force and pound-mass (lbm) for mass; the mechanical equivalent of heat is 1 Btu = 778 ft-lbf.

At which temperature are the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales numerically equal?
