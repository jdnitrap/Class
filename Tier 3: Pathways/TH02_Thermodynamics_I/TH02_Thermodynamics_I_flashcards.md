# TH02_Thermodynamics_I - Quick Reference Flashcards

## Flashcard ID: 1
### Question: What is the First Law of Thermodynamics?

Energy conserved: E_in - E_out = ΔE_system.

---

## Flashcard ID: 2
### Question: What is the six-step energy conversion in power systems?

Heat input → Fluid heating → Turbine work → Generator electricity → Condenser cooling → Pump pressure increase.

---

## Flashcard ID: 3
### Question: What is the energy balance for power systems?

Heat in from source = Electrical power out + Heat rejected.

---

## Flashcard ID: 4
### Question: What is stored energy in a system?

Potential Energy, Kinetic Energy, Internal Energy, Flow Energy.

---

## Flashcard ID: 5
### Question: What is transient energy?

Energy in transit: Heat (temp difference) and Work (force).

---

## Flashcard ID: 6
### Question: What are sign conventions for heat and work?

Q added: positive, Q removed: negative. W by system: positive, W on system: negative.

---

## Flashcard ID: 7
### Question: What is potential energy?

Energy from position. PE = m × z × g / gc.

---

## Flashcard ID: 8
### Question: What is kinetic energy?

Energy from velocity. KE = m × v² / (2 × gc).

---

## Flashcard ID: 9
### Question: What is internal energy (U)?

Sum of all microscopic energy in substance. Changes with temperature/pressure.

---

## Flashcard ID: 10
### Question: What is flow energy (PV energy)?

Energy from pressure × volume. Specific: P × v. Work to push fluid in/out.

---

## Flashcard ID: 11
### Question: What is enthalpy (H)?

H = U + PV. Total energy content of fluid.

---

## Flashcard ID: 12
### Question: What is specific enthalpy (h)?

h = u + Pv. Energy per unit mass (Btu/lbm). Used in all cycle calculations.

---

## Flashcard ID: 13
### Question: How is enthalpy used in turbine calculations?

Work = h_in - h_out. (Enthalpy drop).

---

## Flashcard ID: 14
### Question: How is enthalpy used in pump calculations?

Work = h_out - h_in. (Enthalpy rise).

---

## Flashcard ID: 15
### Question: What is heat (Q)?

Transient energy due to temperature difference. Flows hot → cold. Not a property.

---
