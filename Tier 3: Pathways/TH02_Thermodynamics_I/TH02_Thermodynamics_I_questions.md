### Question
Heat and work are best classified as which type of energy?

### Explanation
Heat and work are transient energy forms—energy in transit. They exist only during energy transfer between systems and surroundings.

Which equation correctly defines enthalpy (H)?

### Question
Flow work (PV energy) is the energy a fluid possesses due to:

### Explanation
Flow energy (PV) is the work required to push a fluid of volume V at pressure P into a system. It equals P×v per unit mass.

### Question
In thermodynamic sign conventions, when heat is removed from a system, Q is considered:

### Explanation
Sign convention: Q added (into system) = positive; Q removed (out of system) = negative. Similarly, W done by system = positive, W done on system = negative.

### Question
When work is done ON a system (compression), the work value is considered:

### Explanation
Sign convention: Work done BY the system (expansion, turbine) = positive. Work done ON the system (compression, pump) = negative.

### Question
In a steady flow process, what remains constant by definition?

### Explanation
Steady flow: constant mass flow rate in/out, constant properties at each location over time, constant heat/work transfer rates. Most power equipment operates in steady flow.
