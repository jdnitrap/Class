### Question
What is the primary functional difference between a safety valve and a relief valve?

### Explanation
Safety valves are designed to fully open at the setpoint with little/no accumulation and close sharply below setpoint (blowdown); relief valves open gradually as pressure rises, with the fully-open point defined as accumulation.
