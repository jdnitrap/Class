How is the state of a substance defined?
By two independent intensive properties; once two properties are known, all others can be determined.

[Note ID: 2]
What are the four phases of matter?
Solid (fixed shape/volume), Liquid (fixed volume, takes container shape), Vapor/Gas (no fixed shape/volume), Plasma (ionized gas).

[Note ID: 3]
What is melting and vaporization?
Melting: Solid to Liquid (absorbed heat). Vaporization: Liquid to Gas (absorbed heat).

[Note ID: 4]
What is condensation and solidification?
Condensation: Gas to Liquid (rejected heat). Solidification: Liquid to Solid (rejected heat).

[Note ID: 5]
What are the three boundary lines on a phase diagram?
Fusion line (Solid-Liquid), Vaporization line (Liquid-Gas), Sublimation line (Solid-Gas).

[Note ID: 6]
What is the triple point on a phase diagram?
The point where all three phases (solid, liquid, gas) coexist in equilibrium.

[Note ID: 7]
What are the five states of water?
(1) Subcooled liquid, (2) Saturated liquid, (3) Wet vapor (mixture), (4) Saturated vapor, (5) Superheated vapor.

[Note ID: 8]
What is subcooled liquid?
Water below saturation temperature, resistant to vaporization, nearly incompressible.

[Note ID: 9]
What is saturated liquid and saturated vapor?
Saturated liquid: at saturation temperature, on verge of boiling. Saturated vapor: 100% vapor, last drop just evaporated.

[Note ID: 10]
What is wet vapor?
Mixture of saturated liquid and vapor; both phases coexist at the same temperature.

[Note ID: 11]
What is sensible heat?
Heat addition/removal that changes temperature without causing phase change.

[Note ID: 12]
What is latent heat?
Heat addition/removal that causes phase change at constant temperature; all energy goes into changing molecular structure.

[Note ID: 13]
What is latent heat of vaporization (Hfg)?
Energy needed to convert saturated liquid to saturated vapor; same energy released when vapor condenses.

[Note ID: 14]
What is steam quality (X)?
The fraction of vapor in a wet mixture; ranges from 0 (all liquid) to 1.0 (all vapor).

[Note ID: 15]
What quality steam is required at turbine inlet and why?
High quality (>95-99%) required because low quality causes blade erosion and corrosion.
