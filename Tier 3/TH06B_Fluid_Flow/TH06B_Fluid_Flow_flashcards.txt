[Note ID: 1]
What is mass flow rate and its formula?
ṁ = ρ × V̇ = ρ × v × A (lbm/hr); where ρ = density, v = velocity, A = cross-sectional area.

[Note ID: 2]
What is volumetric flow rate and common units?
Q = V̇ = v × A; common units: gallons per minute (gpm) or cubic feet per minute (cfm).

[Note ID: 3]
What is continuity equation?
Mass flow rate into a system equals mass flow rate out: ṁ₁ = ṁ₂.

[Note ID: 4]
What is viscosity and how does temperature affect it?
Fluid's resistance to flow; for liquids: decreases with increasing temperature; for gases: increases with increasing temperature.

[Note ID: 5]
What is specific gravity (SG)?
Ratio of fluid density compared to water at 60°F; water = 1.0; SG < 1 floats, SG > 1 sinks.

[Note ID: 6]
What is fluid density and typical value for water?
Mass per unit volume; water ≈ 62.4 lbm/ft³ at standard conditions; density changes with temperature and pressure.

[Note ID: 7]
What is laminar flow?
Occurs when Reynolds number < 2,000; fluid layers move smoothly with minimal mixing; parabolic velocity profile.

[Note ID: 8]
What is turbulent flow?
Occurs when Reynolds number > 3,500; characterized by cross-currents and mixing; flattened velocity profile; requires more energy.

[Note ID: 9]
What is transitional flow?
Occurs when Reynolds number is between 2,000-3,500; unstable, alternates between laminar and turbulent.

[Note ID: 10]
What is Reynolds number and its formula?
NRe = (ρ × v × d) / μ (dimensionless); determines flow regime (laminar vs. turbulent).

[Note ID: 11]
What is single-phase flow?
Fluid completely in either liquid OR gaseous state, not a mixture; easier to analyze and predict.

[Note ID: 12]
What is two-phase flow?
Fluid mixture of liquid AND vapor phases; creates much greater resistance; head loss increases by factor of 100+.

[Note ID: 13]
What is elevation head (z)?
Potential energy component of Bernoulli's equation; height above reference point; same regardless of path.

[Note ID: 14]
What is velocity head?
Kinetic energy component of Bernoulli's equation; v²/(2gc); height fluid would rise if all kinetic energy converted to potential.

[Note ID: 15]
What is pressure head (Pv)?
Pressure-volume energy component; height of fluid column whose weight equals the fluid pressure.

[Note ID: 16]
What is total head?
Sum of elevation head + velocity head + pressure head; constant in ideal flow (Bernoulli equation).

[Note ID: 17]
What is friction head loss (Hf)?
Conversion of fluid pressure/velocity to heat energy through friction; expressed in feet of head.

[Note ID: 18]
What is friction factor (f)?
Dimensionless constant in Darcy's equation; accounts for pipe surface roughness; depends on Reynolds number.

[Note ID: 19]
What is cavitation in piping?
Vapor bubbles form when local pressure drops below saturation pressure; causes serious pump impeller damage.

[Note ID: 20]
What is choked flow?
In compressible flow (steam), velocity reaches Mach 1; pressure wave forms that limits mass flow; Pout/Pin ≈ 0.5.

[Note ID: 21]
What is water hammer?
Pressure surges when flow suddenly changes; can spike to 20× normal pressure; causes pipe damage and injury risk.

[Note ID: 22]
What is pump head (Hp)?
Energy added to fluid by pump; Wp = ṁ × Hp × gc/g.

[Note ID: 23]
What is continuity equation for variable pipe diameter?
v₁ × A₁ = v₂ × A₂; smaller diameter = higher velocity; larger diameter = lower velocity.

[Note ID: 24]
How does velocity change with pipe diameter?
When diameter increases, velocity decreases proportionally; when diameter decreases, velocity increases proportionally.

[Note ID: 25]
What is Bernoulli's equation?
z₁ + v₁²/(2gc) + Pv₁ = z₂ + v₂²/(2gc) + Pv₂ (total head constant in ideal flow).
