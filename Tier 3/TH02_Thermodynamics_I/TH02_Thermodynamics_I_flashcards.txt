[Note ID: 1]
What is the First Law of Thermodynamics?
Energy conserved: E_in - E_out = ΔE_system.

[Note ID: 2]
What is the six-step energy conversion in power systems?
Heat input → Fluid heating → Turbine work → Generator electricity → Condenser cooling → Pump pressure increase.

[Note ID: 3]
What is the energy balance for power systems?
Heat in from source = Electrical power out + Heat rejected.

[Note ID: 4]
What is stored energy in a system?
Potential Energy, Kinetic Energy, Internal Energy, Flow Energy.

[Note ID: 5]
What is transient energy?
Energy in transit: Heat (temp difference) and Work (force).

[Note ID: 6]
What are sign conventions for heat and work?
Q added: positive, Q removed: negative. W by system: positive, W on system: negative.

[Note ID: 7]
What is potential energy?
Energy from position. PE = m × z × g / gc.

[Note ID: 8]
What is kinetic energy?
Energy from velocity. KE = m × v² / (2 × gc).

[Note ID: 9]
What is internal energy (U)?
Sum of all microscopic energy in substance. Changes with temperature/pressure.

[Note ID: 10]
What is flow energy (PV energy)?
Energy from pressure × volume. Specific: P × v. Work to push fluid in/out.

[Note ID: 11]
What is enthalpy (H)?
H = U + PV. Total energy content of fluid.

[Note ID: 12]
What is specific enthalpy (h)?
h = u + Pv. Energy per unit mass (Btu/lbm). Used in all cycle calculations.

[Note ID: 13]
How is enthalpy used in turbine calculations?
Work = h_in - h_out. (Enthalpy drop).

[Note ID: 14]
How is enthalpy used in pump calculations?
Work = h_out - h_in. (Enthalpy rise).

[Note ID: 15]
What is heat (Q)?
Transient energy due to temperature difference. Flows hot → cold. Not a property.
