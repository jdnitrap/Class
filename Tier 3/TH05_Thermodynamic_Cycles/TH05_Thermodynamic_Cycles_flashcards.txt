What are the five essential elements of a thermodynamic cycle?
Working fluid, Engine (turbine/piston), Heat source (boiler), Heat sink (condenser), Pump/compressor.

[Note ID: 2]
What is cycle efficiency?
η = (Q_added - Q_rejected) / Q_added = net work / heat added.

[Note ID: 3]
What is the Carnot cycle?
Theoretical max efficiency. Four processes: isothermal expansion, adiabatic expansion, isothermal compression, adiabatic compression.

[Note ID: 4]
What is Carnot efficiency dependent on?
Only absolute temperatures (K) of source and sink. Independent of fluid type.

[Note ID: 5]
How does isothermal expansion occur in Carnot?
Constant temperature. Heat absorbed from source. Pressure ↓, Volume ↑. Produces work.

[Note ID: 6]
How does adiabatic expansion occur in Carnot?
No heat transfer. Temperature and pressure ↓. Fluid expands, extracting maximum work.

[Note ID: 7]
What is the Rankine cycle?
Realistic steam cycle for actual power plants. Four main processes.

[Note ID: 8]
What are the four Rankine processes?
Pump compression (adiabatic), Heat addition (isobaric boiler), Turbine expansion (adiabatic), Heat rejection (isobaric condenser).

[Note ID: 9]
How is pump compression in Rankine?
Adiabatic. Increases liquid pressure. Requires work input.

[Note ID: 10]
How is heat addition in Rankine?
Constant-pressure. Boiler raises enthalpy. Liquid → saturated or superheated steam.

[Note ID: 11]
How is turbine expansion in Rankine?
Adiabatic. Pressure/enthalpy ↓. Produces useful work output.

[Note ID: 12]
How is heat rejection in Rankine?
Constant-pressure. Condenser removes latent heat. Steam → liquid.

[Note ID: 13]
What is net work in Rankine?
W_net = W_turbine - W_pump. Pump work only 1-5% of turbine work.

[Note ID: 14]
How does Rankine compare to Carnot?
More realistic (isobars vs isotherms). Easier to analyze. Lower efficiency but practical.

[Note ID: 15]
Primary method to increase heat source temp?
Superheat steam in boiler. Increases energy input. Larger temp difference. Limited by material strength.

[Note ID: 16]
Primary method to decrease heat sink temp?
Improve condenser cooling. Deep vacuum maximizes efficiency. Limited by ambient temp.

[Note ID: 17]
How do irreversibilities affect Rankine efficiency?
Real turbines/pumps add entropy from friction losses. Reduces efficiency vs ideal cycle.

[Note ID: 18]
Effect of pump work on Rankine?
Pump uses 1-5% of turbine output. Net work = turbine - pump.

[Note ID: 19]
How does condenser vacuum improve efficiency?
Lower pressure/temp increases temp difference source-sink. Improves Carnot efficiency.

[Note ID: 20]
What fluid is used in Rankine for power plants?
Steam (water). Abundant, good properties. Absorbs/rejects large heat quantities.
